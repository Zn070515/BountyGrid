"先知平台关于AI生成漏洞报告的处置公告\n随着大模型能力日益增强，利用AI工具辅助或自动化挖掘漏洞已成为安全研究的新趋势。近期，我们确实收到了一些由AI协助发现的高质量漏洞报告，但同时也面临大量未经人工验证的无效报告，甚至是被AI“幻觉”误导而产生的误报。\n\n为了合理分配有限的审核资源，确保先知审核团队能聚焦于真实有效的漏洞，同时维护良好的社区生态，特此发布本公告，对AI辅助挖掘和生成的漏洞报告提交标准进行规范：\n\n●\n鼓励AI使用：先知平台积极鼓励白帽子利用AI工具辅助或自动化挖掘漏洞，提升发现漏洞的效率，挖掘更多潜在风险。\n\n●\n严格人工验证：对于AI辅助或自动化挖掘产出的漏洞报告，提交前务必进行人工验证。请确保您已完成对报告真实性与危害性的评估和复现，并在报告中详细提供人工验证结果，包括但不限于：\n\n○\n漏洞危害说明\n\n○\n详细复现步骤\n\n○\n完整的POC（请避免提供All In One形式的POC）\n\n○\n关键步骤及结果截图\n\n●\n无效报告处理：对于直接由AI生成、未经人工复现或未提供有效验证结果截图的报告，平台将直接予以驳回，且不提供驳回原因说明。\n\n●\n违规账号处置：对于累计提交超过3个AI生成报告且未经上报者人工验证报告有效性的白帽子，平台将提醒您的行为；对于累计提交超过5个AI生成报告且未经上报者人工验证报告有效性的白帽子，平台有权拉黑您的先知账号。\n\n我们深知AI技术正在深刻改变安全研究的方式与效率，但高质量的漏洞报告目前还离不开人的判断与责任感。希望广大白帽子在享受技术红利的同时，严守技术伦理，坚守质量底线，共同维护一个专业、高效、可持续发展的漏洞披露与交流平台。\n\n感谢您一直以来对先知平台的支持与信任！\n\nAs AI capabilities continue to grow, leveraging AI tools to assist with or automate vulnerability discovery has become a new trend in security research. Recently, we have indeed received several high‑quality vulnerability reports identified with AI assistance; however, we are also confronted with a large number of invalid reports that have not been manually verified, and even false positives generated by AI “hallucinations.”\n\nTo allocate limited review resources effectively, ensure that the Xianzhi Triage Team can focus on genuine, actionable vulnerabilities, and maintain a healthy community ecosystem, we hereby issue this notice to standardize the submission criteria for AI‑assisted and AI‑generated vulnerability reports:\n\n●\nEncouraging AI Use: The Xianzhi platform actively encourages white hats to use AI tools to assist in or automate vulnerability discovery, thereby improving efficiency and uncovering more potential risks.\n\n●\nRigorous Manual Verification: For vulnerability reports produced through AI assistance or automated discovery, please conduct thorough manual verification before submission. Ensure that you have assessed and reproduced the report’s authenticity and severity, and provide detailed results of your manual validation in the report, including but not limited to:\n\n○\nA description of the vulnerability’s impact\n\n○\nStep-by-step reproduction instructions\n\n○\nA complete PoC\n\n○\nScreenshots of key steps and outcomes\n\n●\nHandling Invalid Reports: Reports directly generated by AI, without prior manual reproduction or valid verification screenshots, will be rejected outright, with no explanation provided for the rejection.\n\n●\nAction Against Violating Accounts: White hats who submit more than 3 AI‑generated reports without having their validity confirmed through manual review will receive a warning. Those who submit more than 5 such reports without manual validation may have their account suspended.\n\nWe recognize that AI technology is profoundly transforming the methods and efficiency of security research; however, high‑quality vulnerability reports still rely on human judgment and responsibility. We hope that all white hats, while benefiting from technological advances, will uphold ethical standards, adhere to quality benchmarks, and work together to maintain a professional, efficient, and sustainable platform for vulnerability disclosure and collaboration.\n\nThank you for your continued support and trust in the Xianzhi platform!\n\n参与该处置公告的SRC组织：阿里云先知平台、蚂蚁SRC、腾讯SRC、百度SRC、字节SRC、京东SRC、滴滴SRC、美团SRC、快手SRC、深信服SRC、携程SRC、顺丰SRC、度小满SRC、BOSS直聘SRC、荣耀SRC、智联招聘SRC、科大讯飞SRC、中通SRC、小红书SRC、货拉拉SRC、小拉SRC、小鹏汽车SRC、理想汽车SRC、奇富SRC、陌陌SRC、vivoSRC、360SRC、OPPO安全中心、小米安全中心、补天漏洞响应平台、360漏洞云、BUGBANK、太初众测平台（排名不分先后）\n\n​"
